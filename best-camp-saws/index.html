Quick answer

The Bahco 396-LAP Laplander folding saw is the best camp saw for most people, blending a sharp, reliable blade with a comfortable grip at a reasonable price.

If you want to cut faster and are willing to spend more, get the Silky Gomboy; for budget-friendly reliability choose the Corona RS 7265D; for splitting firewood grab the Fiskars Pro; and for ultralight backpacking the Gerber Freescape is your best bet.

A good camp saw turns a chore into a quick task. Whether you’re bucking firewood for a campfire, clearing trail blowdown, or trimming tent stakes, a folding saw is lighter and safer than an axe and easier to pack than a full-size bow saw. The key trade-offs are blade length, tooth geometry, weight, and lock security. Longer blades cut bigger logs but add heft; aggressive tooth patterns (like impulse-hardened or triple-ground) cut faster but can bind in green wood. A sturdy lock keeps the blade from folding on you, and a comfortable handle matters when you’re making dozens of cuts. Our verdict

For most campers, the Bahco 396-LAP Laplander is the obvious choice: it’s versatile, reliable, and still affordable. If you value speed and are ready to spend more, the Silky Gomboy is a joy to use.
