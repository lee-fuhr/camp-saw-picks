Quick answer

The Bahco 396-LAP Laplander is our top pick, a reliable, affordable folding saw that handles most camp tasks with ease.

The Silky GOMBOY is the premium choice for serious woodcutters, while the Gerber Freescape is the lightest and most packable option for backpackers.

A good folding camp saw is one of those pieces of gear you don’t think about until you need it, and then you really need it. Whether you’re processing firewood, clearing trail debris, or cutting tent poles, a saw that won’t bind, won’t rust, and packs small is worth its weight in gold. The right one balances tooth sharpness, blade length, handle comfort, and folded size, and the best models hold an edge long enough to make camp chores fast and safe. How to choose? Start with your typical use. If you’re car camping and splitting bigger logs, you want a longer blade (around 8 inches) with aggressive teeth. If you’re backpacking, weight and packed length matter most. Look for a lockable blade (a safety latch is non-negotiable), a comfortable grip that won’t blister, and a sheath or folding mechanism that protects both you and the saw. These three picks cover the full range, from budget-friendly workhorses to ultra-premium cutters to minimalist trail tools.

Best for specific needs

Best for all-around camp chores

When you need to process larger diameter logs or you’re spending a week in the backcountry processing wood for warmth, speed and sharpness matter most. Every gram counts on a long trail, and the Gerber Freescape was designed with that mantra in mind. While it won’t fell a tree, it easily handles trail-clearing tasks and small-diameter firewood, making it a worthy companion for minimalists. Our pick: Gerber Freescape Camp Saw.

Our verdict

Choosing the right folding camp saw comes down to how you camp. For most people, the Bahco 396-LAP Laplander is the obvious starting point, it does everything well at a fair price. If you cut wood every trip and want the fastest, sharpest blade available, step up to the Silky GOMBOY. And if you’re a gram-counting backpacker who just needs a reliable saw for the trail, the Gerber Freescape will lighten your load without leaving you stuck. Any of these three will serve you well; pick the one that best fits your pack and your plans.
